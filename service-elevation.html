House Elevations —
Protect Your Home from Flood Risk

FEMA-approved elevation work serving Marshfield, Scituate, and the South Shore coastline. We handle engineering, permitting, and construction — start to finish.

If your home is in a flood zone, elevating it above the Base Flood Elevation (BFE) is one of the most financially and structurally sound investments you can make. A properly completed elevation can dramatically reduce your annual flood insurance premiums, increase your home's value, and protect it from damage in the severe storms that hit our coastline every few years.

Biviano General Contracting has extensive experience performing FEMA-approved house elevations in Marshfield and Scituate — two of the most flood-impacted communities on the South Shore. We handle every step of the process, from the initial site assessment and FEMA elevation certificate through engineering, permitting, foundation work, and final inspection.
