High-Quality Decks Built
Fast, Clean, and Built to Last

From demolition to final inspection, Biviano GC manages every step. Premium materials. No drama. Done in 2–4 days.

A new deck should enhance your home, last for decades, and be built without stress — and that's exactly what we deliver. Biviano General Contracting specializes in high-quality deck construction with fast 2–4 day build times, clean workmanship, and a streamlined process that takes the hassle off your plate.

From removing your old deck to handling permits, framing, decking, railings, and final inspections, our crew manages every step with precision and care. We use premium materials like Trex, TimberTech, and pressure-treated lumber, ensuring durability, safety, and long-term performance. With decades of local experience and hundreds of decks built across the South Shore, we're known for reliability, no-drama communication, and craftsmanship that truly stands out.
