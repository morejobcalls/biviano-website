No Guesswork

Our 9-Step Process to
A Perfect Project

From first call to final walkthrough — here's exactly what happens when you work with Biviano GC. No surprises. Just a clear, proven process built over 40+ years.

1

Site Evaluation & Initial Consultation

We come to you — no charge, no obligation. We walk the property, discuss your goals, and assess feasibility. You leave with a clear picture of what's possible before spending a dollar.

2

Design Consultation

We work with you on the design — layouts, materials, finishes, and scope. For larger projects, we coordinate with engineers and architects so the design is buildable, permitted, and within budget from the start.

3

Engineering & Permitting

We handle all engineering plans, structural calculations, and permit applications. We manage the town's building department process so you don't have to navigate the bureaucracy yourself. Projects don't start until all permits are in hand.

4

Project Proposal & Pricing

You receive a detailed, itemized proposal with clear scope, materials, and pricing. No vague estimates. No surprise line items later. What we quote is what you pay — unless you request changes to the scope.

5

Contract & Deposit

Once you're ready, we sign a clear contract outlining all work, timeline, payment schedule, and warranty terms. A deposit secures your spot on our schedule. Financing options are available if needed.

6

Material Selection & Ordering

We guide you through material selections — decking, roofing, cabinetry, flooring, fixtures, and more. Materials are ordered and staged before your start date so there are no delays once work begins.

7

Demolition & Site Prep

Our crew handles all demolition, debris removal, and site preparation — clean, safe, and respecting your property. We protect surrounding areas, minimize mess, and keep your neighbors at ease throughout.

8

Construction

Our experienced crew executes the build with precision. You receive regular progress updates, and we're always reachable if you have questions. We maintain a clean, organized job site from first day to last.

9

Final Walkthrough & Warranty

We do a thorough final walkthrough together. Every detail is reviewed, every concern is addressed before we consider the job complete. You receive your 20-year craftsmanship warranty documentation, and we stay in touch. Our relationship doesn't end when the project does.
