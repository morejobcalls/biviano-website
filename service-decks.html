Outdoor Living, Done Right

A new deck, built without the stress

A new deck should enhance your home, last for decades, and be built without stress — and that's exactly what we deliver. Biviano General Contracting specializes in high-quality deck construction with fast 2–4 day build times, clean workmanship, and a streamlined process that takes the hassle off your plate. From removing your old deck to handling permits, framing, decking, railings, and final inspections, our crew manages every step with precision and care. We use premium materials like Trex, TimberTech, and pressure-treated lumber, ensuring durability, safety, and long-term performance. With decades of local experience and hundreds of decks built across the South Shore, we're known for reliability, no-drama communication, and craftsmanship that truly stands out.

20× Longer Than Required

The 20-Year Craftsmanship Warranty

Biviano GC offers a 20-year craftsmanship warranty on every deck we build — not one year, not five, but twenty. That's twenty times longer than what Massachusetts requires, and we offer it because our decks are built to last. The only thing that will age over time is the wood itself — our craftsmanship and structural work are solid for decades.

Most contractors avoid long warranties because they rush jobs, cut corners, or use cheaper materials. We don't. We've lived and worked in Marshfield our entire lives, and our office is right in town. We stand behind our work because we're not going anywhere.

If you want a deck built by a company that backs its work for 20 years, give us a call. We're here in Marshfield — and here to stay.
