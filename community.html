Our History

Four Generations in Marshfield

The Biviano family has been part of the Marshfield community since the 1960s. What started as a trade passed down through a family has grown into one of the South Shore's most trusted contracting companies — built one project, one relationship, one generation at a time.

We're not a faceless corporation. We're your neighbors. The homes we work on are on the same streets where our kids play, where we shop, where we go to church. That proximity keeps us honest, and that connection is why we take every project personally.

Marshfield Planning Board

The Biviano family's commitment to this community goes beyond the projects we build. Michael Biviano Sr. served on the Marshfield Planning Board for years, helping shape the growth and development of the town he loved. His dedication to responsible, thoughtful development is a value we carry forward in everything we do.

When we advise homeowners on their projects, we do it with a full understanding of what makes this community special and how to build in a way that respects the neighborhoods we've called home our entire lives.

In Loving Memory

Michael Biviano Sr.

Our father, founder, and the foundation of everything Biviano GC stands for. Mike Sr. built this company on the values of respect, fairness, honesty, and taking pride in your work — values that have been passed down through every generation. He believed that a contractor's reputation was their most valuable asset, and he never compromised on that principle, not once.

He served the Marshfield community not just through his work, but through his civic involvement, his generosity, and his genuine love for the people and the place he called home. Every project we complete is a reflection of his standard and a tribute to his legacy.

RIP, we love you and miss you every day.
