Improve The Way You Live

Renovations that blend seamlessly with your home

Renovations and additions should improve the way you live — not add stress — and that's why homeowners across the South Shore trust Biviano General Contracting with their most important projects. From kitchen and bathroom upgrades to full home makeovers, added living space, dormers, in-law suites, and second-story expansions, we handle every phase with precision and care. Our team manages the entire process, including design guidance, permits, engineering, demolition, framing, mechanicals, finishes, and inspections, ensuring your project moves smoothly from concept to completion. With decades of experience and a deep understanding of local building codes, zoning requirements, and coastal regulations, we create renovations and additions that blend seamlessly with your home while delivering long-term value, comfort, and craftsmanship.
