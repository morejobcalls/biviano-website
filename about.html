The Biviano Family

Building Marshfield
Since the 1960s

The Biviano family has been part of Marshfield's building and real estate community since the 1960s. What began as a family trade passed down through generations has grown into a trusted South Shore name for quality construction, renovations, modular homes, decks, and coastal elevation projects.

Owner Michael Biviano Jr. spent years working alongside his father, completing countless homes, additions, kitchens, decks, and structural projects across Marshfield, Duxbury, and Scituate. Today, he continues that legacy with a focus on professionalism, integrity, and lasting results.
